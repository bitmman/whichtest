## Tests

- `one_sample_t` **One-sample t-test** (ready): Compare the mean of one continuous measure with a fixed reference value: a norm, a target, or a historical mean. Checks: normality, outliers. Fallback: one_sample_wilcoxon.
- `indep_t` **Independent-samples t-test (Welch)** (ready): Compare the means of a continuous outcome between two independent groups. Checks: normality_by_group, independence, variance_info. Fallback: mann_whitney.
- `oneway_anova` **One-way ANOVA** (ready): Compare a continuous outcome across three or more independent groups. Checks: residual_normality, equal_variance. Fallback: kruskal.
- `factorial_anova` **Factorial ANOVA** (judgment): Two or more grouping factors, such as treatment × sex, with a continuous outcome and their interaction. Checks: residual_normality, cell_variance, balance. Fallback: robust_or_permutation. Decisions required: ss_type, include_interaction.
- `simple_lin_reg` **Simple linear regression** (ready): Model a continuous outcome as a linear function of one continuous predictor. Checks: linearity, residual_normality, homoscedasticity, influence. Fallback: spearman.
- `multiple_reg` **Multiple linear regression / ANCOVA** (ready): Continuous outcome with several predictors, or a group comparison adjusted for covariates (ANCOVA). Checks: residual_normality, homoscedasticity, multicollinearity, homogeneous_slopes. Fallback: ols_hc3.
- `paired_t` **Paired t-test** (ready): Two measurements on the same subjects (pre and post, left and right) or matched pairs, with a continuous outcome. Checks: difference_normality, pair_alignment. Fallback: wilcoxon_signed.
- `rm_anova` **Repeated-measures ANOVA** (helper): Three or more measurements per subject on one within-subject factor, with every subject complete. Checks: sphericity, balance, residual_normality. Fallback: friedman.
- `lmm` **Linear mixed model** (judgment): Repeated or clustered continuous outcomes with missing visits, uneven timing, between-subject factors, or nesting such as patients within sites. Checks: random_effects, convergence, residual_normality, cluster_count. Fallback: gee_cont. Decisions required: re_formula, reml.
- `gee_cont` **GEE for a continuous outcome** (judgment): Clustered or longitudinal continuous data where you want population-averaged effects rather than subject-specific ones. Checks: cluster_count, working_correlation, missingness. Fallback: lmm. Decisions required: working_correlation.
- `wilcoxon_signed` **Wilcoxon signed-rank test (paired)** (ready): Paired or matched measurements when the differences aren't normal, or the outcome is ordinal. Checks: symmetry, zero_handling. Fallback: sign_test.
- `friedman` **Friedman test** (ready): Three or more repeated conditions per subject with an ordinal or non-normal outcome and complete data. Checks: complete_blocks. Fallback: ordinal_gee.
- `ordinal_gee` **Ordinal GEE** (judgment): An ordinal outcome measured repeatedly or within clusters, with covariates. Checks: proportional_odds, cluster_count, sparse_levels. Fallback: gee_logit. Decisions required: working_correlation.
- `one_sample_wilcoxon` **Wilcoxon signed-rank (one sample)** (ready): Compare the center of one ordinal or non-normal measure with a reference value. Checks: symmetry. Fallback: sign_test.
- `mann_whitney` **Mann–Whitney U test** (ready): Two independent groups with an ordinal outcome, or a continuous outcome that isn't normal. Checks: shape_similarity. Fallback: brunner_munzel.
- `kruskal` **Kruskal–Wallis test** (ready): Three or more independent groups with an ordinal or non-normal continuous outcome. Checks: shape_similarity.
- `spearman` **Spearman rank correlation** (ready): Monotonic association between two ordinal or non-normal continuous variables. Checks: monotonic. Fallback: kendall_tau.
- `ordinal_logit` **Ordinal logistic regression** (helper): An ordinal outcome with several predictors and independent observations. Checks: proportional_odds, sparse_levels. Fallback: mnlogit.
- `mcnemar` **McNemar's test** (ready): A binary outcome measured twice on the same subjects, or in matched pairs. Checks: paired_table, discordant_count.
- `cochran_q` **Cochran's Q test** (ready): A binary outcome under three or more conditions on the same subjects, with complete data. Checks: complete_blocks. Fallback: gee_logit.
- `gee_logit` **Logistic GEE** (judgment): A binary outcome measured repeatedly or within clusters, such as patients within clinics, with covariates. Checks: cluster_count, working_correlation, estimand. Fallback: mixed_logistic. Decisions required: working_correlation.
- `binom_test` **Exact binomial test** (ready): Compare one observed proportion with a reference proportion. Checks: independence.
- `two_by_two` **Chi-square or Fisher's exact (2×2)** (ready): A binary outcome compared between two independent groups. Checks: expected_counts.
- `chi_sq_indep` **Chi-square test of independence** (ready): Two categorical variables with independent observations, such as group by outcome category. Checks: expected_counts. Fallback: permutation_chi_square.
- `logistic` **Logistic regression** (ready): A binary outcome with a continuous predictor or several predictors, independent observations. Checks: events_per_variable, separation, logit_linearity, multicollinearity. Fallback: firth_logistic.
- `stuart_maxwell` **Stuart–Maxwell test** (ready): A categorical outcome with three or more levels measured twice on the same subjects (marginal homogeneity). Checks: square_table.
- `nominal_gee` **Nominal GEE** (judgment): An unordered categorical outcome measured repeatedly or within clusters. Checks: cluster_count, sparse_levels. Decisions required: working_correlation.
- `chi_gof` **Chi-square goodness-of-fit** (ready): One categorical variable compared with an expected distribution. Checks: expected_counts. Fallback: exact_multinomial.
- `mnlogit` **Multinomial logistic regression** (ready): An unordered categorical outcome with three or more levels and predictors. Checks: iia, events_per_category.
- `gee_poisson` **Poisson GEE** (judgment): A count outcome measured repeatedly or within clusters, such as monthly ED visits per patient. Checks: exposure_offset, cluster_count, working_correlation. Decisions required: working_correlation.
- `poisson_2indep` **Two-sample rate comparison** (ready): Compare event rates between two groups, accounting for different follow-up time. Checks: event_independence. Fallback: poisson_reg.
- `poisson_reg` **Poisson or negative binomial regression** (ready): A count outcome with predictors, optionally with an exposure offset. Checks: overdispersion, excess_zeros. Fallback: zip.
- `zip` **Zero-inflated count model** (judgment): Counts with more zeros than Poisson or negative binomial predicts, and a plausible reason for structural zeros, such as never-users. Checks: zero_process, beats_nb. Fallback: hurdle_model. Decisions required: count_distribution, inflation_formula.
- `km_logrank` **Kaplan–Meier with log-rank test** (ready): Compare time to event between groups, without adjustment, with censoring. Checks: noninformative_censoring, crossing_curves. Fallback: rmst.
- `cox` **Cox proportional hazards** (helper): Time to event with covariates, to estimate hazard ratios. Checks: proportional_hazards, events_per_variable, covariate_linearity. Fallback: cox_cluster.
- `competing` **Competing risks analysis** (judgment): More than one event type can end follow-up, such as death before readmission. Checks: estimand. Fallback: fine_gray. Decisions required: estimand, event_of_interest.
- `cox_cluster` **Clustered or stratified Cox model** (judgment): Time to event with subjects nested in sites or families, or a covariate that breaks proportional hazards. Checks: cluster_count, frailty_vs_robust. Decisions required: approach.
- `manova` **MANOVA** (helper): Compare groups on several continuous outcomes at once, such as a panel of related lab values. Checks: multivariate_normality, covariance_homogeneity, outcome_redundancy. Fallback: pillai_trace.
- `mancova` **MANCOVA** (helper): Several continuous outcomes compared between groups, adjusted for covariates. Checks: multivariate_normality, covariance_homogeneity, homogeneous_slopes.
- `mv_reg` **Multivariate multiple regression** (helper): Predict several continuous outcomes from the same set of continuous predictors. Checks: multivariate_normality, multicollinearity.
- `factor_sem` **Factor analysis or SEM** (judgment): The outcomes are items of a scale or indicators of an underlying construct, such as questionnaire domains. Checks: approach, sample_size, factorability. Decisions required: approach.
- `pearson` **Pearson correlation** (ready): Linear association between two continuous variables. Checks: linearity, outliers, bivariate_normality. Fallback: spearman.
- `point_biserial` **Point-biserial correlation** (ready): Association between a binary variable and a continuous one. Checks: normality_by_group, equal_variance. Fallback: mann_whitney.
- `partial_corr` **Partial correlation** (helper): Association between two continuous variables after removing the effect of one or more others. Checks: linearity, residual_normality.
- `cohen_kappa` **Cohen's kappa** (ready): Two raters assign categories to the same items. Use weighted kappa for ordinal ratings. Checks: same_categories, prevalence.
- `fleiss_kappa` **Fleiss' kappa** (ready): Three or more raters assign categories to the same items. Checks: complete_ratings.
- `bland_altman` **Bland–Altman analysis** (helper): Two methods measure the same continuous quantity, such as a new device against a reference. Checks: difference_normality, proportional_bias. Fallback: log_limits.
- `icc` **Intraclass correlation (ICC)** (judgment): Reliability of continuous ratings across raters, or across repeated measurements. Checks: icc_form, residual_normality. Decisions required: icc_form.
- `diag_binary` **Sensitivity, specificity and predictive values** (helper): A binary test result compared with a gold-standard diagnosis. Checks: verification, prevalence.
- `roc_auc` **ROC curve and AUC** (helper): A continuous score or predicted probability compared with a binary gold standard. Checks: positive_class, ci_method.
- `delong` **DeLong test for paired AUCs** (helper): Two scores evaluated on the same patients: is one AUC larger? Checks: same_patients, not_nested.
